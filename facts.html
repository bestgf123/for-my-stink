🌎 Birthday Traditions Around the World

Because apparently there is no single correct way to celebrate surviving another year.

🇲🇽

Mexico

Birthday celebrations can feature piñatas, and Las Mañanitas is traditionally sung around birthdays. Some celebrations also include the famous cake-face tradition.

🇩🇪

Germany

A common superstition says that wishing someone a happy birthday before the actual day brings bad luck. So September 6? Absolutely not.

🇨🇳

China

Birthday celebrations may include long noodles associated with longevity. The idea is to symbolize a long life.

🇧🇷

Brazil

In some Brazilian birthday traditions, the first slice of birthday cake is given to the person the birthday celebrant considers especially loved or important.

🇦🇺

Australia

Fairy bread — buttered white bread covered in colourful sprinkles — is a classic Australian children's party food.

🇨🇦

Canada

Some Canadian birthday traditions involve putting butter on the birthday person's nose as a playful superstition intended to ward off bad luck.

📜 September 7: On This Day

Your birthday apparently has quite a lot going on historically.

1533

Elizabeth I was born

Elizabeth I was born at Greenwich Palace on September 7, 1533.

1776

A submarine attacked a warship

The American submarine Turtle attempted to attach a time bomb to a British warship in New York Harbor. It is generally regarded as the first submarine attack in warfare.

1813

"Uncle Sam" appeared in print

The nickname "Uncle Sam" was printed in the Troy Post in New York during the War of 1812 era.

1927

Electronic television took a huge step forward

Philo Farnsworth successfully demonstrated his image dissector, an important milestone in the development of fully electronic television.

1936

The last known thylacine died

The last known thylacine, commonly called the Tasmanian tiger, died in captivity at Hobart Zoo in Tasmania.

1940

The Blitz began

German bombing of London and other British cities intensified on September 7, beginning the period commonly known as the Blitz.

1963

The Pro Football Hall of Fame opened

The Pro Football Hall of Fame opened in Canton, Ohio, with 17 charter members.
