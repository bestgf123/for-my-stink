What Your Birthday Says About You

Some of this is astronomy, some is tradition, and some is basically the universe gossiping.

♍

You're a Virgo

September 7 falls under Virgo. Traditionally, Virgo is associated with being observant, practical, analytical, organised and ridiculously good at noticing things everyone else missed.

Your Birthstone: Sapphire

September's traditional birthstone is sapphire. Sapphire is a variety of corundum and is famous for its blue colour, although sapphires can occur in other colours too. Traditionally, sapphire has been associated with wisdom, sincerity and protection.

🌸

Your Birthday Flower: Aster

Aster is one of the traditional September birth flowers. The name comes from the Greek word for "star", because the flower's shape resembles a little star. Traditionally, asters have been associated with love, wisdom and faith.

🌔

Your Moon: Waxing Gibbous

On September 7, 2003, the Moon was a Waxing Gibbous. It was roughly 90% illuminated and was getting very close to the Full Moon, which occurred on September 10.

In moon-lore, a waxing gibbous is often associated with growth, momentum and getting very close to the thing you want.

Birthday Madness Around the World

Because apparently saying "happy birthday" normally was never enough.

🇧🇷

Brazil

In some Brazilian birthday traditions, the birthday person can have their earlobes pulled once for every year they've been alive. Congratulations: your birthday has become a physical endurance event.

🇩🇰

Denmark

Danish birthday traditions can involve putting a flag outside the home. So instead of wondering whether someone is having a birthday, Denmark basically puts up a giant spoiler.

🇯🇲

Jamaica

One Jamaican birthday tradition involves covering the birthday person in flour. Imagine waking up excited for cake and discovering that your friends have chosen violence.

🇲🇽

Mexico

The piñata turns birthday celebrations into a bizarre combination of party, treasure hunt and controlled destruction. Hit the colourful object until sweets fall from the sky.

🇦🇷

Argentina

Birthday celebrations can involve throwing flour and sometimes eggs at the birthday person. Basically, cake is optional. Getting covered in kitchen ingredients is not.

🇰🇷

South Korea

Seaweed soup is traditionally associated with birthdays. So while everyone else is thinking "birthday cake", someone has decided "excellent, soup".

On This Day

September 7 has had a surprisingly chaotic history.

1822

Brazil declares independence

Pedro, the Prince Regent of Brazil, declared independence from Portugal. September 7 is now celebrated as Brazil's Independence Day.

1921

The first Miss America pageant

The first Miss America competition was held in Atlantic City. Which means your birthday shares a date with the beginning of a very long-running American tradition.

1923

INTERPOL is founded

The organisation that eventually became INTERPOL was established in 1923. So technically, September 7 has international detective energy.

1940

The Blitz begins

Nazi Germany began a major bombing campaign against Britain during the Second World War. This is one of the more serious events associated with the date.

1963

The Pro Football Hall of Fame opens

The Pro Football Hall of Fame opened in Canton, Ohio. We're leaving the sports statistics out of this page, but the building apparently deserves a birthday mention.

1996

Tupac is shot in Las Vegas

On September 7, 1996, Tupac Shakur was shot in Las Vegas. He died six days later.
